Averaging

Scans per spectrum

Each spectrum is the mean of several scans: noise falls with √N, updates get slower.

Dark background

Even in darkness the detector reads a background of counts. Measure it once at the current exposure and it is subtracted from every spectrum.

Scans to average

Smoothing (boxcar)

Half width px

Each pixel becomes the mean of its neighbours: less noise, but lines narrower than the window get wider.
